Full-Service HVAC Care for Fort Wayne Homes and Businesses

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: aging furnaces on the older streets, newer heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and rooftop units on commercial buildings. Our team handles all of it. We have years of field experience, and we know how the local climate, the housing stock, and local code affect the way a system performs over time.

Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs tend to be quick: a failed capacitor, a dirty flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling itself to an early death. Tune-ups include coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend the life of your system.

Commercial service is a big part of what we do as well. We service rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment for restaurant kitchens, shops, offices, and multi-unit properties, knowing that a day without cooling hurts the business. To keep that downtime short, we keep common commercial parts stocked, diagnose fast, and plan repairs around your operating hours whenever possible.

Two common situations call for quick action from you before our technician gets there. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can overflow the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Fort Wayne

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
