How Evansville Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
