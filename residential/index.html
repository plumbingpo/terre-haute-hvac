Maintenance Plans

Maintenance Plans for Terre Haute Homes

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service prevents failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still cheap to fix. At Terre Haute HVAC, every plan is built around your home's actual equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check across the duct system.

Most homes do best with two visits a year: a spring tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating. We adjust the schedule to the age of your equipment, how hard your household works it, and local conditions like dust and pollen, which clog condenser coils and raise energy use fast.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a regular maintenance schedule cuts operating costs. A clean coil transfers heat far more efficiently, a properly charged system stops overworking the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Most families find that a maintenance plan costs far less than replacing an HVAC system even one year early.

Sized for Your Home

The Right Size System for Your Home

An undersized system runs nonstop and never catches up on the hottest days, while an oversized one short-cycles, wastes energy, and leaves the house humid. Terre Haute HVAC sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual home. That means a proper load calculation based on square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout, rather than simply matching the tonnage of the old unit. For ductless mini-splits, it means matching head capacity to each zone so every room holds its setpoint.

We walk you through the options for most homes, especially growing families with rising comfort demands. A single-stage system that served a small household rarely satisfies a finished basement and two added bedrooms, and a modern two-stage or variable-speed heat pump can hold tighter temperatures while cutting energy use dramatically. If you're weighing furnace-and-AC against a heat pump, or ducted against ductless, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: SEER2 efficiency ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting requirements, and upfront installation work, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Repairs We Handle

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Terre Haute

No cooling, a furnace that won't ignite, a frozen evaporator coil, leaking refrigerant: each of these problems has a different cause and needs a different repair. Terre Haute HVAC diagnoses the exact fault before swapping any parts, whether it's a condenser that hums but won't start, a short-cycling furnace, a thermostat that loses its settings, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split throwing error codes.

Some problems are mostly a nuisance, while others pose real risks. A weak capacitor simply keeps the compressor from starting, but a cracked heat exchanger can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling. We match the fix to the failure: capacitors, contactors, and fan motors on cooling systems, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards on furnaces, sealed and repaired ductwork where conditioned air is bleeding into the attic, and a proper refrigerant recharge after a repaired leak. Whatever you're facing, our team has the parts and experience to handle it.
